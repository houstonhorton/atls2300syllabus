At Recitations:
The LA will be responsible for delivering a
synopsis of the course. You will receive a handout.
You will pick up an assignment that you can
perform during that period. Sign and place the
assignment in the folder on your way out of the
room.
Each recitation assignment is also an attendance
mechanism.
If the recitation has a reading and discussion
assignment. This Happens during the session and
the text is read out loud with everyone present.
Each person reads one or two paragraphs.
Each person writes a 2 sentence synopsis.
by hand.
Each recitation has a practice which you can
participate with as long as you please.
If it is a quiz the quiz can rerefer to any source. All
quizzes are to be handed in before you leave.
The work you make must be handed in before you
leave the room.
There are no late assignments for the recitations
don’t ask for extensions due to absence.
10 Assignments will be accepted of a possible 15
assignments during the semester.

deck. Each deck is from a di erent author

This is an assignment at the intersection of
speculative futures and storytelling
using—more or less—accepted forms
of source material and retold and cited for
the purposes of promoting a
favorable/unfavorable circumstance and
the consequences that are in the truth of
this ‘fact’. Numbers are central to this
work. There must be a number story.
Use AI at your own risk. AI errors are
common, if the AI errors are present in
your finished work. Your final grade for the
assignment will drop by a full letter grade.
EXAMPLE:
A 4 Slide deck produced from a piece of
information found in an ESPN report
about the inbound trend of ‘Leisurewear’.
The statistic might support investment in a
‘breathable textile startup’. The author
might be a tech/textile company selling
the investment opportunity. Located in
Jaipur, Northern India. With more than one
technical fabric in its portfolio.
